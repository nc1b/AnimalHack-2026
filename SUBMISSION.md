## 2) Arcade video — how to record it

[Arcade](https://www.arcade.software/) records an *interactive* demo by
capturing your browser as you click through the live site. I can't drive your
Arcade account, but here's an exact recipe.

**Setup**
1. Log in at arcade.software → **New Arcade → Record → Chrome extension**.
2. Open the live site: https://pawfinder-animalhack.vercel.app
3. Record at 1280×800+ for a crisp result.

**Shot list (≈75 seconds — hits every judging beat)**

| # | Action on screen | Say / caption |
| - | ---------------- | ------------- |
| 1 | Land on the home page; let the floating hero cards animate | "Every year millions of pets go missing — and the clues to find them are scattered everywhere. PawFinder puts them on one map." |
| 2 | Click **Live Map**; click the **Mochi** (red) pin | "Every pin is a real report — lost in red, found in green." |
| 3 | In the side panel click **View full report** | "Here's Mochi, a lost Shiba in a red harness…" |
| 4 | Point at the **✨ Likely matches — 73%** card | "…and PawFinder already found a 73% match: a tan dog in a red harness spotted under the Maple Park bandstand." |
| 5 | Click **⬇ Alert poster** → the PNG downloads | "One click makes a printable alert poster to rally the neighborhood." |
| 6 | Close; click **Report lost pet**; fill a couple fields; click the map to drop a pin; **Post report** | "Reporting takes under a minute — add a photo, drop a pin, done. No account needed." |
| 7 | Show the auto-opened match / toast | "New reports are matched instantly." |
| 8 | Back to Live Map, click the purple **Buddy** pin (reunited) | "And when a pet gets home, we celebrate it. Bring them home — with PawFinder." |

**After recording:** Arcade → **Share → Publish** → copy the public Arcade link.
Paste that link into Devpost's video field (Arcade links embed on Devpost).
