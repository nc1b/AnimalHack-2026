How PawFinder works

1

Report in a minute

Add a photo, drop a pin where the pet was last seen, and describe them. No account needed.

2

We match automatically

Our matcher scores every lost report against every found sighting by species, color, and distance.

3

Alert the neighborhood

Generate a printable alert poster in one click and share it wherever your community looks.

4

Mark reunited

When a pet gets home, close the report so the map stays accurate and hopeful.
